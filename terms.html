Using the site

2trapz games is free for personal fun. By using it you agree to these terms and the Privacy policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. Keep your password to yourself and don't pretend to be someone else. Pick a gamertag that's friendly. We may change rude gamertags or remove accounts that break these rules.

Reviews & messages

  • Be respectful. No bullying, hate, swearing, threats or spam.
  • Don't post personal information (yours or anyone else's), like addresses, phone numbers or passwords.
  • Reviews should be about the game.
  • 2trapz decides which reviews appear and can remove any review at any time.
  • By posting a review, you let 2trapz show it on the site.

Submitting games

  • Only send games you made, or that you have permission to share.
  • You keep ownership of your game. If 2trapz adds it, you let 2trapz host it and show it on the site, with credit, until you ask for it to be taken down.
  • No viruses, nothing harmful, and nothing that isn't OK for a general audience.
  • 2trapz picks which games go on the site.

Fair play

Achievements, TZ and the Ranks are just for fun. Messing with the site to fake scores or break things can get your account removed from the leaderboard.

The games on 2trapz games belong to their creators. Don't copy or re-upload them somewhere else as your own.
