# Roadmap

## Known gaps
- AI racers follow the route kinematically; they never crash or spin.
- No damage model beyond a counter; no weather (rain, wipers) yet.
- Force feedback is impossible in browsers; only gamepad rumble.
- Rally field times are simulated from the reference car; tune `diffK` in `js/race.js` if stages feel too easy or hard.

## Ideas
- Rain with windscreen droplets and wipers.
- Tandem drift battles against AI.
- Replays with free camera.
- Photo mode in the garage.
