# Game modes

| Mode | Where | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Story | Port Vesper, rally stages, Shin-Kurogane | Clear chapter events; see [Story](story.md) |
| Rally | 5 stages | Beat a simulated field of 8 rally drivers; AI, human or no co-driver |
| Time Trial | Rally stages + city circuits | Medals (platinum, gold, silver, bronze) and a saved ghost of your best run |
| Drift | Docks, Neon Mile, Kurogane backstreets, Akagiri Pass | Score in 90 s, 2 min or 3 min; combo multiplier up to 6x |
| Free Roam | Any map, any time of day, traffic on/off | Explore; drift combos pay cash; ramps in the docks |

Story events add **sprint**, **circuit**, **highway duel** (gap the rival by N metres), **drag** (perfect-shift bonus), **touge** (1v1 on the mountain pass) and **time attack**.

## Rewards
Story pays the event reward on the first win and 30 % on replays. Arcade modes pay by finishing position, medal or drift score.
