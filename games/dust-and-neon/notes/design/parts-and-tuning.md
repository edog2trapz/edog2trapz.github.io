# Parts and tuning

## Engine
Intake (short ram, **cold air intake**, **headlight intake** that removes a headlight at night, ram-air scoop), exhaust (cat-back, fart can, **straight pipe** with pops, quad, side-exit, titanium, takeyari boso pipes), forced induction (small turbo, big turbo with lag, twin turbo, **roots blower through the hood**, centrifugal supercharger), **nitrous** (single, twin, purge kit), ECU (stage 1, stage 2, custom laptop map, anti-lag), internals (forged, stroker), lightweight flywheel.

## Drivetrain and chassis
Close-ratio box, sequential dog box, open / 1.5-way / 2-way / welded diff, AWD or RWD conversion, suspension (sport, coilovers, rally long-travel, air bags), brakes with caliper colour, six tyre compounds, weight reduction, roll cages.

## Body, lights and interior
Body kits (street, widebody, time attack, rally, bosozoku, Kitsune), spoilers, hoods, eight rim styles with colour, liveries with number and colour, paint with six finishes, headlight colour, rally light pod, window tint, **neon underglow** (static, pulse, rainbow, strobe, rpm), **interior LEDs** and starlight headliner, steering wheels, shift knobs, bucket or carbon seats, harnesses, gauge pods, mirror charms, and the **tuning laptop**.

## Engine swaps
Any engine into any chassis (except hypercar powertrains); weight follows the engine. The RB-26 unlocks after beating Hayato.

## Laptop tuning
Requires the laptop. Boost, rev limiter, final drive, brake bias, diff lock, steering lock, aero balance, and with adjustable suspension: ride height, camber, spring stiffness. A live dyno graph redraws as you move sliders. With a custom map flashed, press K in the car to cycle Eco / Street / Race / Attack maps; the in-cabin laptop shows the live ECU screen.
