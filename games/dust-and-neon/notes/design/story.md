# Story

An original street-racing story set in Port Vesper. You arrive to work for **Ren Okabe**, a retired tuner whose career was ruined by a rigged race. **Mika Sato**, hacker and co-driver, lives on her ECU laptop. Progress is gated by car class (D, C, B, A, S, X), so the garage is where races are won.

| Chapter | Gang / setting | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Fresh Plates | Gutter Kings, docks | First races in the Civica, laptop ECU tutorial, Dez's dare |
| 2 Venom | Vipers (JDM, green neon) | Drift initiation, highway duel with Jin, Sora Vance boss; Viper livery |
| 3 Iron & Fire | Iron Saints (V8 muscle) | Boulevard drag, pink slip vs Big Moss for his Hellfire |
| 4 Dust Run | Dustwolves rally series | Gifted Fjord Rally2; Mika as co-driver (or a friend); Anika Brandt |
| 5 Rising Sun | Kitsune-kai, Japan | Shin-Kurogane city, Akagiri touge, Bayline duel, Hayato Kirishima; RB-26 and Kitsune aero unlocks |
| 6 Blacklight | Blacklight Syndicate | Neon Gauntlet, Prism, Ren's lap record, final pink slip vs Vex's Valkyr hypercar |

Dialogue uses procedurally drawn portraits in gang colours. Content lives in `js/data.js` (`D.STORY`, `D.CHARS`, `D.GANGS`).
