# Cockpit and driver

First person is the default camera. The cabin has a dashboard, a live gauge cluster (analogue or digital race dash with shift lights), a rear-view mirror rendered from a rear camera, inner door panels, headliner, roll cage, passenger seat, nitrous bottles when fitted, the tuning laptop, LEDs and a swinging mirror charm.

## Animations
- Arms are two-bone IK chains from the shoulders; gloves grip the rim and follow it up to about 110°.
- **Manual H-pattern**: the inboard hand leaves the wheel, the knob moves through the gate to the new gear, and the hand returns. Sequential cars pull and push the lever; DCT cars flick paddles.
- **Handbrake**: the hand grabs the lever (hydraulic stick in rally cars) and the lever pulls.
- Head motion: g-force lean, braking pitch, look-to-apex, bumps from rough surfaces, mouse or stick look, look-back.
- In rally, a helmeted co-driver sits in the passenger seat reading the notes.

## Driver customization
Name, crew tag, jacket style and colours, **gloves** (racing gauntlet, fingerless, leather, carbon knuckle, mechanic, bare), headwear (a full-face helmet adds a visor frame to the first-person view), hair and skin. Gloves and sleeves show on your arms in the car.
