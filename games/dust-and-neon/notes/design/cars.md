# Cars

All makes are fictional. Class from the stock build; upgrades move cars up.

| Class group | Cars |
|---|---|
| JDM | Hoshida Civica EK (Civic-style starter with the laptop, RHD), Tsuruga Hachi 86, Mazura RX-Spirit (rotary), Tsuruga Supremo RZ, Nisaka Raijin R34 (AWD) |
| Drift | Nisaka Sylph S5, Bavaria M-Sport 46 |
| Rally | Kazemori WRX STR, Mitsuboshi Lanza Evo, Fjord Rally2 Hatch (sequential, anti-lag, light pod), Lancera Deltaforza S4 (Group B twin-charged) |
| Muscle | Dominion Hellfire (supercharged V8) |
| Super | Pfalz 9R GT, Moretti Furia V10, Rossetti 488 Scuderia |
| Hyper | Valkyr Aether, Veyrona Quattro W16, Voltara Nevera EV (single-speed, quad motor) |

JDM cars are right-hand drive in the cockpit. Bodies are generated from side profiles (`js/carmodel.js`), so every car has wheel arches, a greenhouse, a hood that opens over a modelled engine bay, and per-part visuals.
