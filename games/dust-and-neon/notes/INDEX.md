# Dust & Neon

A browser 3D racing game with two halves: **rally stages** where a second player is your co-driver (the driver gets no map), and a **street-racing story** in neon cities with gangs, rivals, pink slips and deep car customization.

Play it on 2trapz games, or open `../index.html` through a local web server (see [How to run](how-to-run.md)).
