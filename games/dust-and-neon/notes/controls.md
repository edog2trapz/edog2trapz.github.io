# Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Wheel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Throttle / brake | W / S (or arrows) | RT / LT | pedals (bind) |
| Steer | A / D | left stick | wheel axis (bind, 180–1080°) |
| Handbrake | Space | A or X | bind a button |
| Nitrous | Left Shift or N | B | bind |
| Shift up / down (manual) | E / Q | RB / LB | paddles, or H-shifter mode |
| Clutch (clutch kicks) | Left Ctrl | — | clutch pedal |
| Camera (cockpit, hood, bumper, chase, far) | C | Y | bind |
| Reset to road (+5 s in rally) | R | Back | bind |
| Lights | L | D-pad up | — |
| ECU map switch (needs laptop + custom map) | K | D-pad down | — |
| Look left / right / back | Z / X / B, or drag the mouse | right stick, stick clicks | — |
| Big map (not in rally stages) | M | — | — |
| Horn | H | — | — |
| Toggle auto / manual | T | — | — |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start | bind |

Holding the brake at a standstill engages reverse in both transmission modes. Before the green light the clutch is held in, so throttle revs the engine for a launch.
