Tricks and tips

  • Tap jump to hop. Hold it in the air to backflip.
  • Land belly-flat along the slope. Every landed trick gives you a speed boost, and chaining tricks builds a combo.
  • Hold trick in the air to strike a pose. Let go before you land or you wipe out.
  • Hold trick on the ground to tuck and go faster downhill.
  • Fish add boost. Seals are trampolines. Snowmen smash. Jump over ice blocks, rocks and logs.
  • Water costs you time. Jump the gaps.
  • Rocket start: press jump right as GO appears.
