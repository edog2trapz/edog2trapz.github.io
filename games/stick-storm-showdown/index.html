High, mid, low

  • High attacks miss anyone crouching.
  • Mid attacks hit crouchers. Block them standing (hold back).
  • Low attacks hit standing guards. Block them by crouching.
  • Throws beat blocking, but crouching makes them miss.
  • Sidestep to dodge straight attacks and the camera swings around.

Combos

  • Back + Punch is a launcher. Keep hitting while they float.
  • Forward + Kick pushes rivals into the ring wall for a wall splat.
  • Hit someone starting an attack for a counter (bonus damage).
  • Gamepad: X punch, A kick, B ability, Y throw, RB parry, LB dodge, RT ultimate.

Stick Brawl abilities

  • Ability recharges on its own. Watch the bar under your energy.
  • Ultimate needs a full meter. It's armored and lands a 6-hit cinematic.
  • Parry right before a hit: no damage, they stagger, and projectiles bounce back.
  • Dodge rolls through attacks. Hold Up or Down to roll sideways.
  • Rhythm combos: after a hit, attack again in the green zone for PERFECT. Mashing breaks the chain. Four PERFECTs fire a Combo Finisher.
  • Drop below 35% health and you Awaken: more damage, faster ability.
  • Arena events: low gravity, speed frenzy, double knockback, eruptions and supply drops.
