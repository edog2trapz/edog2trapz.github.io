# Dead Rude design notes

The design bible for **Dead Rude**, the first-person horror game on 2trapz games. Open this folder in Obsidian ("Open folder as vault"), or read it on GitHub: every link is a plain relative markdown link, so it works in both.

You play Dex Marlowe, the rudest ghost hunter on the internet. Alive, you investigate ghosts with real kit and survive their hunts. Each ghost you banish leaves its power in you, until you become a ghost yourself and can cross the Veil.

## Notes

- [Game modes](modes.md): Story, Contract, Escape and Classic, and how each one starts, plays and ends.
- [Ghost types](ghost-types.md): the ten types, their evidence, their bane and how they behave. Read this to win contracts.
- [Evidence and tools](evidence-and-tools.md): EMF reader, spirit box, thermometer, UV light, obsidian mirror, flashlight, and how each evidence shows up.
- [Smokeables](smokeables.md): every Geekbar model and flavour, the THC carts, joints and cigars, what they do and which bane they count as.
- [Houses](houses.md): Blackthorn Manor, Hollowmere Farmhouse and St. Agnes Asylum, room by room.
- [Controls](controls.md): keyboard, mouse and touch, alive and dead.
- [Code map](code-map.md): where everything lives in `dev/src/` and how to rebuild the game.
