# Evidence and tools

Evidence goes into the journal automatically the moment you witness it. You can also tap an evidence line to mark it yourself, or cross it out. The ghost types list fades out every type your marks rule out.

| Slot | Tool | What it does | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | EMF reader | Five LEDs. Spikes near anything the ghost touched in the last 20 s, and reads 2-3 near the ghost itself. Beeps faster as it climbs. | **EMF Level 5**: about 38% of a type's touches read 5 |
| 2 | Spirit box | Click or T to ask a (rude) question. Answers in a radio voice (text-to-speech, can be turned off in Settings). Works with the flashlight **off**, within about 8 m. | **Spirit Box** |
| 3 | Thermometer | Reads the room you are standing in. The ghost's room cools over a couple of minutes, and your breath shows below 0°C. | **Freezing Temps**: below 0°C in its room |
| 4 | UV light | Click to switch on. Shows glowing handprints on doors and light switches the ghost touched. | **UV Handprints** |
| 5 | Obsidian mirror | A black volcanic-glass scrying mirror. Hold click to look into the Veil: furniture lifts, and the ghost shows through walls. Costs sanity and cracks after 20 s of use in total. | none, it finds the ghost |
| 6 | Smoke | Your current smokeable. See [Smokeables](smokeables.md). | **Smoke Faces**: a face forms in a cloud blown within about 6.5 m of the ghost (9 m with the Obsidian Edition) |
| F | Flashlight | Left hand. Casts real shadows on High graphics. Flickers when a ghost is close. Counts as electronics during hunts. | |

## Also in the house

- **Light switches** beside the door of every room. The dark drains sanity; a lit room does not.
- **Fuse box** near the front door. Hunts sometimes trip the power.
- **Tethers**: the cursed object a ghost cannot let go of, glowing faintly in its room. Blow its bane over it, with the right name in the journal, to banish it.
- **Ghost activity** without evidence: doors creaking and slamming, lights switching, furniture jolting, thrown books and plates, knocks and whispers, and full apparitions.

## Sanity

Starts at 100. Only drains in the dark (faster on harder contracts and near a Hollow), and drops when a ghost shows itself. Smoking brings it back. Below about 35% the screen closes in and hunts get likely. Not used in Escape mode.
