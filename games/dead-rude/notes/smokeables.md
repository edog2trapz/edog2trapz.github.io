# Smokeables

Hold R (or right click, or click with slot 6) to draw, release to blow a cloud. Hold longer than about 1.25 s for a big cloud. Defined as `SMOKES` and `FLAVOURS` in `dev/src/02b-hunt.js`.

Every cloud does three things: brings back sanity (alive), chokes any ghost that walks through it, and counts as a [bane](ghost-types.md#banes) when blown over a tether.

## Devices

| Item | Kind | Cloud | Uses | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Geekbar | vape | 3.2 m, 5 s | 7% juice a hit | +4 sanity, short nicotine rush (+6% speed) |
| Geekbar Mini | vape | 2.5 m, 4 s | starts at 60% | small and cheap |
| Geekbar Banshee 40K | vape | 4.3 m, 6.5 s | 10% a hit | huge clouds |
| Geekbar Obsidian Edition | vape | 3.4 m, 6 s | 7% a hit | black glass shell; smoke faces show from further away and last longer |
| Graveyard Kush cart | THC cart (indica) | 3 m | 9% oil a hit | +14 sanity, **Couch-locked** 45 s: 20% slower, sanity barely drops |
| Purple Poltergeist cart | THC cart (hybrid) | 3 m | 9% a hit | +12 sanity, **Mellow** 45 s |
| Sour Spectre cart | THC cart (sativa) | 3 m | 9% a hit | +16 sanity, **Paranoid** 45 s: 10% faster, but you see shadow people and hear footsteps that are not there |
| Joint | weed | 3.4 m, 7 s | 6 hits | +10 sanity, **Mellow** 45 s: quieter footsteps, slower sanity loss |
| Cuban cigar | tobacco | 6 m, 14 s | 8 hits | thick smoke a hunting ghost cannot see through; 38% chance you cough (loud) |

Joints and cigars are lit with a lighter on the first hit. Pods (+40% juice) refill your emptiest vape.
