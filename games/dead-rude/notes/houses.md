# Houses

Each house is a character grid (one character = 2 m) in `MAPS` in `dev/src/02a-maps.js`. `#` wall, `.` corridor, `+` door, `L` locked door, `W` Veil ward, `=` the Landlord's seal, letters are rooms. Rooms are furnished automatically by type, except the original Blackthorn rooms, which are placed by hand.

## Blackthorn Manor

51 x 27 cells. Story, Classic, and also playable in Contract and Escape.

- Original rooms: Foyer, Great Hall (sealed in Story/Classic), Library, Study (locked), Dining Room, Kitchen, Portrait Gallery, Master Bedroom, Nursery (locked), Music Room, Conservatory.
- East wing (new): a 50 m east corridor, the Chapel (pews, altar, confessional to hide in), Bathroom, Billiard Room, Servants' Quarters.
- Light: candles, chandeliers, braziers, fireplaces.

## Hollowmere Farmhouse

21 x 19 cells. A ring of hallways around the kitchen and Grandpa's Study (locked since 1987, and in Escape the Front Door Key is in there). Master Bedroom, Lily's Room, Bathroom, Kitchen, Pantry, Front Hall, Living Room. Floral wallpaper, ceiling bulbs. Best house for a first Contract or Escape.

## St. Agnes Asylum

33 x 21 cells. Four cells, Dr. Vane's Office, the Records Room, Ward B (sixteen beds to hide under), the Operating Theatre, the Showers, the Morgue, the Chapel, the Boiler Room (locked) and the Day Room, joined by long tiled corridors. Fluorescent tubes, some broken, some flickering. Barred windows.
