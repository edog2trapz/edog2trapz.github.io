# Game modes

All four modes start from **Play** on the title screen. The mode screen remembers the house and difficulty you picked last time.

## Story: Dead Rude TV

Blackthorn Manor only. Two acts.

**Act 1, alive.** Dex films three episodes, one ghost at a time:

| Episode | Ghost | Room | Tether | Power absorbed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mr. Pemberton Graves, the butler | Dining Room | silver service bell | Butler's Blink (dash becomes a teleport) |
| 2 | Professor Aldous Quill | Library | inkwell and quill | Scholar's Volley (throw spectral books) |
| 3 | Madame Odette, the bride | Master Bedroom | wedding ring box | Bride's Wail (ring that hits every ghost around you) |

Each ghost gets a random [ghost type](ghost-types.md) every run. To banish one: gather [evidence](evidence-and-tools.md), name the type in the journal, then blow its **bane** smoke over its tether. A wrong name or wrong smoke makes it hunt you immediately.

Your gloves turn more ghostly with each power. After the third, Lord Blackthorn appears and kills Dex on air.

**Act 2, dead.** The original Dead Rude: cross the Veil, slap and roast. The remaining tenants (Nanny Grimshaw in the Nursery, Maestro Vittorio in the Music Room) plus squatters, then the Landlord in the Great Hall. Seen from the other side, the ghosts are just sad people in bedsheets. Dying in Act 2 respawns you in the foyer; evicted ghosts stay evicted.

Dying in Act 1 retakes the current episode. Journal evidence is kept.

## Contract

Pick a house and a difficulty. One random ghost (random type, look, name and room) and its tether. Find the room, gather evidence, name it in the journal, leave by the front door and file the report.

| Difficulty | Evidence shown | Sanity drain in the dark | Grace before hunts | Hunt length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amateur | 3 of 3 | slow | 60 s | ~22 s |
| Professional | 3 of 3 | medium | 30 s | ~28 s |
| Nightmare | 2 of 3 (one hidden) | fast | 5 s | ~36 s |

Pay: $120 / $220 / $380 for a correct name, plus a bonus if you banished it. The front door stays locked during a hunt.

## Escape

Granny-style. You wake up in Nanny Grimshaw's house with no kit, only a cheap Geekbar Mini. She is always visible, hums while she patrols, and investigates noise.

- The front door has three locks: **deadbolt** (Front Door Key), **padlock** (Padlock Key), **boards** (Crowbar). Opening them is loud.
- The Front Door Key is behind a locked door. The **Old Key** opens locked doors.
- Noise: running, doors (slamming is worse), creaky corridor boards, coughing on a cigar, taunting.
- If she sees you entering a hiding spot, hiding will not save you.
- Smoke in her face makes her cough and stop (a cigar cloud longer).
- Caught = the day ends and you wake up again with everything you found. Five days, then she keeps you.

| Difficulty | Walk | Run | Hearing | Sight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 1.2 m/s | 2.6 m/s | x0.75 | 15 m |
| Normal | 1.4 m/s | 3.0 m/s | x1 | 19 m |
| Hard | 1.6 m/s | 3.35 m/s | x1.3 | 24 m |

You sprint at 3.65 m/s for about 3.6 s.

## Classic

The original game: dead from minute one in Blackthorn Manor, with all three stolen powers. Evict all five tenants, then the Landlord.
