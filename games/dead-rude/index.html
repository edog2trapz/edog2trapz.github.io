HOW TO BE RUDE

A tenant's guide to being the worst thing that ever happened to this house.

  • In the living world you cannot see ghosts, but they can see you. Geekbar clouds and lightning show them.
  • Cross into the Veil to see them and smack them. Your Veil time runs down over there. Clouds top it up.
  • Roast a ghost until its Dignity breaks. A humiliated ghost takes far more damage.
  • Roast a ghost while it winds up an attack for a Counter-Roast. Smack things they throw to send them back.
  • Some doors are locked for the living but not for the dead. Some wards only exist in the Veil.
  • Fill your Ego and you get a Main Character Moment. Getting hit humbles you.
