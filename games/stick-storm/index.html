Moves

  • Tap attack for a 3-hit combo: jab, jab, kick.
  • Run + attack for a flying kick.
  • Up + attack launches upward; down + attack in the air spikes down.
  • Special costs one energy bar. With a full meter it fires your Ultimate.
  • Slide down walls and jump off them to get back on stage.

Drops

  • Weapons: Katana, Mega Hammer, Blaster, Rockets, Bombs. Attack uses them until they run out.
  • Power-ups: Health, XL size, Turbo speed, Shield, Energy.
  • Gamepads work too: A jump, X attack, B special.
  • Phones and tablets get on-screen controls.
