# Game Design

## Core loop

1. **Open packs.** Each zone sells a Fossil Pack of 3 cards rolled from that zone's six species.
2. **Place dinos.** Tap an empty pen (or use *Auto-place my best dinos*) to put a dino in it. Each species can be in one pen at a time.
3. **Collect.** Placed dinos fill the zone's chest with coins. The chest stops when full, so you come back and press *Collect*, or hire a ranger to collect for you.
4. **Grow.** Spend coins on more packs, bigger pens, decor, rangers, island upgrades and the next zone.

Spare copies of a card are never wasted: they level the card up, sell for coins, or go to museum collectors for crystals. See [Progression](progression.md).

## Zones

Six zones, unlocked in order: Sunny Shore, Fern Valley, Jungle Ruins, Badlands, Volcano Rim and Frost Peak. Each zone has its own scene, weather, pack, set and chest. A zone starts with 3 pens and expands to 6 (front row first, then the back row).

## The home bonus

A dino earns ×2 in its own zone. Any dino can still go anywhere, so a strong dino from a later zone often beats a weak local one. The pen picker and the card's *Place* screen show the exact income change for each choice, and *Auto-place* solves the whole island greedily across every open zone.

## Tapping

Tapping a dino makes it jump and roar and pays a tap reward of about 6% of that zone's income plus 1% of total income, raised by Keeper Training. Taps within 0.65 s build a combo up to ×2.5.

## Improvements over a typical card tycoon

The genre's usual version has you walk an avatar around a 3D island to reach each slot, and loads slowly. Dino Dynasty:

- loads instantly as one file and runs on phones and desktops;
- lets you tap pens directly, with one-tap auto-placement;
- animates every dino (walking, blinking, roaring) with visitors, weather and income pop-ups;
- adds card levels, set bonuses, museum requests, rangers and offline earnings, a prestige layer, achievements, daily rewards and a random golden event;
- saves locally, syncs through 2trapz cloud saves and exports a save code.
