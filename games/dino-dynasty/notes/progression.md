# Progression

## Cards and spares

The first copy of a species is the card. Every copy after that is a spare. Spares level the card up (with coins), sell for coins, or go to museum collectors. *Sell extras* keeps the spares needed for the next level and any open museum request.

## Sets

Each zone's five non-Mythic species form a set. Completing it unlocks a *Claim* button in Cards: the zone earns ×2 for good, plus 40 crystals × zone number. Owning a zone's Mythic adds +50% to that zone.

## Museum collectors

Up to three requests at a time, each asking for 1–4 spares of a species you own. A request pays crystals and a few minutes of total income. A new collector arrives every 90 seconds while there is room. Requests can be skipped.

## Crystals

Earned from achievements, sets, collectors, daily rewards and the Golden Pteranodon. Spent on the Royal Fossil Pack (60: five cards from any open zone, the last one Epic or better), Time Warp (40: one hour of income now), Feeding Frenzy (25: ×3 income for two minutes) and doubling offline earnings (15).

## Events and boosts

- **Golden Pteranodon:** flies over every 80–170 s. Tapping it gives coins, a 45 s Frenzy, crystals or a free pack.
- **Rally:** a free ×2 for 3 minutes, ready again 15 minutes after you start it.
- **Supply Drop:** a free pack every 4 hours. Unlocking a zone also gives a free pack.
- **Daily reward:** a 7-day cycle of crystals, coins and packs, ending with a Royal pack.

## Away time

Zones with a ranger keep earning while you are away: 30% of their rate (up to 100% with Night Shift) for up to 2 hours (+1 hour per Night Shift level). Zones without a ranger only fill their chest. A welcome-back screen shows the total and offers to double it for 15 crystals.

## Extinction Event (prestige)

Available once a run has earned 50M coins. It grants `floor(sqrt(run coins / 50M))` Amber. Each Amber gives +10% to all income for good. You keep cards, levels, crystals, achievements and sets; coins, zones, pens, decor, rangers and upgrades reset. The Amber Shop sells Head Start coins, Fossil Broker discounts, Veteran Rangers (rangers in the first two zones) and Crystal Vein (more crystals).

## Achievements

Twenty achievements pay crystals for packs opened, species found, sets, Mythics, card levels, zones, lifetime coins, museum deals, taps, golden catches and the first Extinction.

## Tutorial

Six coach tips run across the top of the island until done or skipped: open the starter pack, place a dino, collect, tap, open more packs, unlock Fern Valley.
