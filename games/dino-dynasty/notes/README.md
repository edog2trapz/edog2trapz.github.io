# Dino Dynasty — Design Notes

An idle tycoon about building a dinosaur park on a small island. You open fossil packs, place the dinos you find in pens across six zones, and collect the coins they bring in. It is a 2trapz games original inspired by the card-collection tycoon genre, with its own name, art, species list and systems. The game is one file: [index.html](../index.html), built from [dev/src](../dev/src/template.html).

- [Game Design](game-design.md): the core loop, zones, pens, cards, the home bonus and what was improved over a typical card tycoon.
- [Species](species.md): all 36 dinosaurs, their zone, rarity and body type.
- [Economy](economy.md): every income formula, price curve and the pacing targets from the balance bot.
- [Progression](progression.md): sets, the museum collectors, achievements, daily rewards, events and the Extinction Event.
- [Art and Audio](art-and-audio.md): how the procedural dinos, island scenes and synth sounds are drawn and played.
- [Build and Deploy](build-and-deploy.md): source layout, the build script, the 2trapz site hooks and how to ship an update.

Version 1 shipped on 2026-11-23.
