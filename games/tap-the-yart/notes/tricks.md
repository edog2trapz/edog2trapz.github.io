# Vape Tricks

Tricks work while you're **holding a hit** (story and freeplay). A trick bar appears above the prompt; on phones it's tappable.

| Key | Trick | What happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | O's | Blow a smoke ring that drifts forward and grows. Three in one hit = TRIPLE O. | 0.11 lungs, a tiny cloud |
| G | Ghost | First press blows a ball of smoke; press again between 0.3 s and 1.5 s to snatch it back. Miss the window and it becomes a cloud. | Nothing if you snatch it (+3 buzz) |
| G after R | Jellyfish | Snatch a ghost while one of your O's is still in the air. | — |
| T | French inhale | Smoke rolls up into your nose. No cloud. +2 buzz. | 0.18 lungs |

Tricks earn rep in freeplay, friends nearby react, and there are jobs for three O's, a ghost and a jellyfish. You're still holding a hit while you do them, so teachers who see you still get suspicious.
