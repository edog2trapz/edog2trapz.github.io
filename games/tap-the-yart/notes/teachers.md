In freeplay each teacher teaches one period (standing at the front, sometimes turning to the board, which is when you cheat) and patrols on duty the rest of the day. The principal never teaches; lift her office keys from behind.

Speeds are in tiles per second. The player walks at 3.0 and runs at 5.0.
