# Levels

| # | Period | Name | Clock | Teachers | Detectors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1st Period | Easy Money | 2:30 | Vance | none (tutorial) |
| 2 | 3rd Period | Double Trouble | 3:00 | Grummond, Vance | west bathroom |
| 3 | Lunch | Coach on Duty | 3:00 | Coach, Vance, Grummond | west bathroom |
| 4 | 7th Period | The Principal | 3:30 | Okafor, Coach, Vance, Grummond | both bathrooms |

Each level unlocks after you clear the one before it.

## Stars
- ★ make it back to class
- ★ at least 40% of the clock left
- ★ never put a teacher on alert (no chase, no alarm, no spotted cloud)

## Map building
Levels are carved from a wall-filled grid with `r()` (rectangles), `s()` (single tiles) and `stalls()`. Tile legend: `.` hallway, `,` bathroom, `D` doorway, `S` usable stall, `|` out-of-order stall, `V` vent, `K` sink, `U` urinal, `F` fountain, `C` your classroom, `c` other classrooms. Lockers are added automatically on hallway walls.
