# Tap the Yart — Design Notes

A first-person 3D stealth game: sneak to the school bathroom, hit your stash until BUZZ hits 100%, hide the clouds, and get back to class before the bell. The more you take, the more fried you get. The game is one file: [index.html](../index.html).

- [Core Loop](core-loop.md): the hit → hold → ghost cycle and how buzz, breath and clouds work.
- [The Stash](stash.md): the five items, how fried each one gets you and what happens when you overdo it.
- [Stealth Rules](stealth-rules.md): how teachers see, hear, get suspicious, search stalls and bust you.
- [Teachers](teachers.md): the four teachers and their stats.
- [Levels](levels.md): the four periods, their layouts, goals and star rules.
- [Controls](controls.md): keyboard and touch controls.
- [Tech Notes](tech-notes.md): how the 3D renderer, audio and level builder are put together.
