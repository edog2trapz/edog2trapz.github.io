# Missions and Daily Events

## Missions
Contacts text you mission offers on Telegrom. Reply **I'm in** to start; the current step shows under the clock (▶) and a marker points to the target. One mission at a time. "MISSION PASSED" pays cash and rep.

| Mission | From | When | Steps | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Pickup | Dazza | 8:52 | Grab the package at the bike racks → hand it to Shae at the back tables. Getting detention while carrying it fails. | $40, 15 rep |
| Initiation | Shae | after The Pickup | Hold a 3 s wheelie within 8 m of Coach Briggs | $30, 20 rep, the Hazers stop jumping you |
| Exam Heist | Kai | recess | Lift Okafor's keys → crack the safe, before the exam ends | $30, 20 rep |
| Paint the Town | Mia | lunch | Tag the gym, the canteen and near the principal's office | $35, 15 rep |
| The Footy Bet | Jacko | Period 3 | $20 stake: kick 4 goals in one go | $60, 10 rep |
| Payback | Priya | Period 4 | Get her calculator off Brayden (pickpocket or beat him) → give it back | 2 cheat sheets, a finished essay, 15 rep |
| Milk | Mum | 2:02pm | Buy milk at the servo | $15 |

Offers expire after 30 game minutes.

## Daily events
Each day rolls its own events (the start-of-day card lists them):
- **Relief teacher:** one teacher is replaced by Mr. Dunn, who sees less, moves slower and searches fewer stalls.
- **Fire drill** at a random time: the whole school goes to the oval for ten minutes. Classes pause. Principal Okafor sweeps the halls and is extra suspicious.
- **Bag checks** for one period: any teacher who gets close searches you and confiscates green, tabs, salvia, bombs, firecrackers and spray (+1 wanted). Use your locker.
- **Food fight** at lunch: food flies in the canteen and every teacher runs there.

## PA announcements
Principal Okafor comments over the PA on the morning, lunch, and things you cause: driving on the oval, floods, the foam, the alarm, the till, her keys, graffiti and 4-star wanted.

## Your locker
Locker #217 is outside Room 103 (yellow sticker). Stash and take items there; it keeps them between days and bag checks can't touch it.

## Rep levels
| Rep | Level | Perk |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Nobody | — |
| 50 | Known | Dazza sells salvia and tabs |
| 150 | Respected | Gangs ignore your tags; friends can distract every 28 s |
| 300 | Legend | Teachers notice you 15% less; +$25 every morning |

Rep comes from jobs, missions, tricks, wheelies, fights and the footy/hoops games.

## Blending in
Standing next to two or more students makes teachers much slower to get suspicious (70% slower when you're doing nothing, 45% when you are). Busy halls at breaks are cover.
