# The Phone

A GTA-style phone that slides up bottom-right (Tab, or the 📱 button). The game keeps running while it's out, and **a teacher who sees your phone in class gets suspicious fast**.

## Apps
- **Telegrom** — chats. Quick replies instead of typing.
- **Jobs** — every job, progress and pay.
- **Map** — the whole school with teachers, friends and drops.
- **Timetable** — today's periods and grades so far.
- **Stash** — cash, what you're carrying, what's unlocked.

## Telegrom contacts
| Contact | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Dazza (the plug) | "What you got?" opens his menu. Pay upfront; ~7 seconds later he leaves a **drop** somewhere on campus (bike racks, back tables, goalposts, behind the gym, car park, west fence). Walk to it and press E. |
| Kai, Mia | "Pull up" (they come and follow you), "Distract a teacher" (sends the nearest teacher to the oval, 55 s cooldown), "Where are the teachers?" |
| Mum | Texts you at lunch and when the school calls about detention. |
| HZP yr10 | Year-group chat that reacts to what you do: tags, cars on the oval, floods, foam, fights, the till. |

Incoming texts show as a notification even when the phone is away. Shae, Jacko and Priya also text you, and contacts send **mission offers** you accept with a quick reply (see [Missions and Daily Events](missions-and-events.md)).
