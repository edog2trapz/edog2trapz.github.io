# The Stash

Every save starts with **only the Gleek Bar**. Everything else is bought from Dazza (in person or by text) and stays unlocked. Switch with the number keys, Q or the mouse wheel (ITEM on phones). In story periods unlocked items refill each level (yart ∞, green 4, tabs 2, salvia 2); in freeplay you get a smaller daily ration and buy more.

| Item | Uses | Buzz per hit | Cloud | Smell radius | Fries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gleek Bar | ∞ (start) | 30 | big, pink | 2.4 tiles | nic +0.32 |
| Yart | ∞ ($35 unlock) | 36 | small | none | nic +0.12 |
| Green | 4 | 45 | biggest | 3.8 tiles | green +0.42 |
| LSD Tab | 2 | 40 (slowly) | none | none | lsd +0.62 (slow onset) |
| Salvia | 2 | 50 | medium | 1.2 tiles | salvia +1.0 |

Smell lets a teacher notice a cloud **without seeing it**, so green and Gleek Bar clouds are dangerous even around corners.

## Getting fried
Each substance has its own meter. They wear off at different speeds: salvia in about 20 s, nic in about 30 s, green slowly, LSD barely at all.

| Meter | What you see | What it does | Overdoing it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | blur, oversaturated warm colours, heavy eyelids, muffled music | slower walking, laggy mouse, sideways drift | over 1.15: **green out**, you collapse for 6 s and groan (noise) |
| LSD | colour cycling, rippling walls, chromatic split, trails, teachers glow | none, but it's hard to read the cones | stays strong for the rest of the period |
| Salvia | reality swirls, zipper bands, sepia, camera roll, FOV warp | left/right and mouse flip above 0.5 | over 1.35: you fall to the floor for 4.5 s |
| Nic | head-rush wobble | none | over 1.0: random coughing fits (noise radius 7) |
