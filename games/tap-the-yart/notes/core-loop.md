# Core Loop

1. **Get to a bathroom.** A floating marker points to the nearest bathroom door.
2. **Hit.** Hold SPACE (or TAP) with the item you picked from [The Stash](stash.md). Lungs fill over 1.4–2.2 s depending on the item. Holding past full for 0.45 s makes you cough. An LSD tab is a single tap: no smoke, no holding.
3. **Hold it in.** On release you hold your breath for 3.2–5.6 s (bigger hit, shorter hold). Running burns breath almost twice as fast. Your cheeks puff and your face goes green.
4. **Ghost it.** Press E:
   - next to a **vent**: silent, no cloud ("GHOSTED");
   - inside a **stall**: flush + exhale, no cloud, but the flush is a noise (radius 6) that brings someone to knock;
   - anywhere else: a visible cloud.
5. If your breath runs out you **cough**: a big cloud plus a loud noise (radius 8) that puts teachers on alert.
6. **Get back to class** with BUZZ at 100% and empty lungs before the bell.

## Clouds
Clouds grow, drift, get pulled toward vents and fade over 11–17 s. A teacher who sees one investigates and goes on alert. A cloud touching a **smoke detector** sets off the alarm, which sends every teacher to it.

Exhaling in a locked stall without flushing leaves the stall **smoky** (it drains slowly). A teacher who knocks on a smoky stall busts you.
