# Freeplay — Hazeport High

A whole school day in one open map (76 × 60 tiles): two corridors of classrooms, a library, science lab, canteen, two toilet blocks, a computer lab, the principal's office, detention, a gym, the oval, back tables, the car park, and across the road the servo and Hazeport TAFE.

## The day
The clock runs from 8:40am to 3:00pm (about 14 real minutes; sitting in class runs 5× faster).

| Time | Period | Where | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8:50 | Period 1 · English | Room 103 (Ms. Vance) | Essay: write it, AI it, or hand in a nerd's |
| 9:50 | Recess | anywhere | canteen open |
| 10:10 | Period 2 · Maths EXAM | Exam Hall 108 (Mr. Grummond) | write, copy a neighbour, phone AI, cheat sheet, stolen answers |
| 11:10 | Lunch | anywhere | canteen open, crowds |
| 11:50 | Period 3 · Sport | Oval (Coach Briggs) | footy kicks grade the period |
| 12:50 | Period 4 · Science | Lab (Mr. Pham) | elephant toothpaste = A |
| 1:50 | Period 5 · Computing | Computer Lab (Mrs. Kowalski) | assignment; AI might get flagged (38%) |
| 2:30 | Home time | front gate | walk out to end the day |

Being outside your class during a class period is **skipping**: teachers who see you ask for a hall pass and will eventually give you detention. During breaks nobody cares where you are.

## Wanted, detention, suspension
- Crimes (tagging, smashing, fights, theft, cars, riding indoors, pickpocketing, making bombs) raise **wanted stars** when a teacher sees them. Nerds sometimes snitch.
- With 2+ stars teachers get suspicious on sight; at 4+ they hunt you.
- A haircut at a bathroom mirror (needs clippers) drops 2 stars and makes teachers forget your face. Wanted fades over time, faster off campus.
- Getting caught = detention: −40 minutes, $10 fine, your held item confiscated. Three detentions = suspended, day over.

## Money and unlocks
You start every save with **$20 and only the Gleek Bar**. Buy the rest from Dazza (in person in the south toilets, or by text — see [The Phone](phone.md)). Anything bought once stays unlocked, in freeplay and in the story periods. Story wins pay $15 + $10 per star.

Ways to make money: jobs, pickpocketing, smashing lockers and vending machines, robbing the canteen till, cracking the principal's safe ($150 + exam answers, needs her keys lifted from her pocket), winning fights, footy and hoops.

## Things to do
- **Vehicles:** e-scooters and e-bikes (bike racks, H2, outside the gym). Hold Shift for a wheelie and keep it in the green band. Hotwire cars in the car park (hold E), do donuts on the oval, drive off campus through the gate. People always dive out of the way.
- **Vandalism:** punch (F) lockers, laptops, toilets, sinks, urinals, vending machines. Broken plumbing floods the floor and teachers slip.
- **Spray paint:** tag walls. Tagging Hazer or Oval Boys turf brings two of them to fight you.
- **Gangs and fights:** the Oval Boys (oval) and the Hazers (back tables). Square up with E, punch with F. Fists only.
- **Friends:** Kai and Mia follow you, shout when a teacher is coming, and send a teacher on a wild goose chase once a minute.
- **Nerds:** cheat sheets $15, done homework $10.
- **Science lab:** stink bombs, smoke bombs (teachers can't see through), elephant toothpaste.
- **Food:** canteen at breaks, the servo any time. Eating in class gets noticed.
- **Off campus:** the servo shop and TAFE. Walking into TAFE ends the day with the TAFE ending.

## Endings (report card titles)
Hazeport Legend (chaos 25+), Model Student (great grades), Ghost (no classes attended), Enrolled at Hazeport TAFE, Suspended, Just Another Day.

Not in the game on purpose: guns, knives and hard drugs.
