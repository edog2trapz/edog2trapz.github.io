🎮 Playing

Is everything really free?

Yes. Every game is free to play, with nothing to buy and nothing to download.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Games run right in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. A recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari works best.

A game won't load or looks broken. What do I do?

Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5 on a computer). Some games, like Power Hungry, need an internet connection to download their map. If it's still broken, report the bug and say which game and device you're on.

Do games work on phones?

Many do, but some games need a keyboard (for example, Penguin Polar Rush lets up to four people share one keyboard). Bigger 3D games run best on a computer.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

/ search games · R random game · S switch DORFic style · G open the Guide · Esc close. There's also a secret code…

🪪 Accounts & progress

Do I need an account?

No. You can play everything as a guest. Your gamertag, achievements and favorites are saved in your browser.

What does signing in do?

Make an account with just a username and password (no email needed). Your progress saves to your account so it follows you to any device, your TZ score goes on the Ranks, and you can post reviews. Go to your account.

I forgot my password. What now?

If you signed up with a username, there's no email to send a reset link to, so write your password down somewhere safe. Forgot it anyway? Message 2trapz with your username and they can remove the old account so you can make it again. Back up with the Memory Card so you never lose progress.

Do my game saves follow me to other devices?

Yes, when you're signed in. Every time you open a 2trapz page or start a game, your game saves sync with your account. Sign in on another device and pick up where you left off. (Give it a second after you finish playing: saves upload when you come back to any 2trapz page.)

How do I add friends?

Open Friends. Share your friend code (it's also on your Player ID card) or your invite link. Your friend types your code, taps Add, and you accept the request.

How do I play with friends?

On the Friends page, press ⚔️ Orb Four next to a friend to challenge them to a live online match. They get a pop-up invite on whatever 2trapz page they're on. You can also press 🎮 Invite to ask a friend to jump into any other game at the same time. Online matches only happen between friends.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Sign in on both devices and it moves by itself. Or use the Memory Card on the account page: save your progress to a file (or copy a save code), then load it on the other device.

Where do my game saves go?

Each game saves inside your browser. If you clear your browser data or switch browsers, those saves stay behind.

What are TZ points and levels?

Achievements are worth TZ points. Every 50 TZ is a new level. See them all in the Trophy room.

How do I change my gamertag or avatar?

Click your gamertag in the top bar (or press G) and type a new one, or use the account page.

How do I delete my data or account?

Signed in: open your account page and use "Delete my account". Guest data: open the Guide, go to Settings and choose "Reset everything". You can also ask 2trapz to delete anything.

⭐ Reviews & contact

Why can't everyone see my review yet?

2trapz checks every review before it goes public, to keep the site friendly and spam-free. You can see your own review right away.

Can I get my own game on the site?

Yes! Use Submit your game. Paste a link where it can be played or downloaded (itch.io, GitHub, Scratch, Google Drive…). You must have made it yourself or have permission.

How do I request a game idea?

Use Request a game and describe it. The best ideas become new 2trapz games.

Will I get a reply?

If you leave your email on the contact form, 2trapz can reply. It's optional.

🌐 The site

What is DORFic?

DORFic (Daylight, Orange, Red, Futurism, Graphic) is a glossy, orange, mid-2000s tech look. It's the style of this whole site. Read more on the About page.

How do I change the style?

Click the swirly swatch button in the top bar, or press S. Pick Daylight, Night, Industrial, Blaze or Aero.

Can I turn the sounds off?

Yes. Open the Guide (G), go to Settings and choose Sounds: Off.

Is my information safe?

Your email is never shown publicly. Read the Privacy policy for exactly what's saved and where.
