Who we are

2trapz games (edog2trapz.github.io) is a free website of browser games made and run by 2trapz. Questions about privacy? Email .

Saved on your device

To make the site work, your browser stores a few things in its own local storage: your gamertag and avatar, achievements, which games you played and how often, your favorites, reviews you wrote, and your style and sound settings. Each game also keeps its own saves in your browser.

This stays on your device and isn't sent to us unless you sign in to an account. You can wipe it any time: open the Guide (press G), go to Settings and choose "Reset everything", or clear your browser's site data.

Accounts

Accounts are optional and run on Google Firebase Authentication. The simplest kind uses just a username and password, with no email address needed. Firebase stores your username and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, which we can never see. If you choose to sign in with an email address, Google or GitHub instead, Firebase keeps that email and how you signed in, and with Google or GitHub we also receive your name and profile picture.

Your profile is saved in Google Cloud Firestore: gamertag, avatar, TZ score and level, achievements, play counts, favorites and style choices. Your gamertag, avatar and scores are public on the leaderboard. Your username, password and email address are never shown publicly.

Reviews

When you post a review we save your name, star rating and text, and email a copy to 2trapz. If you're signed in, the review is linked to your account. Reviews become public only after 2trapz approves them. You can delete your own reviews.

Contact form

Messages, game submissions and game requests are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to 2trapz's email inbox. They include what you typed, your gamertag, and your email only if you chose to give it.

Other services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so your browser connects to Google to load them.
  • Google Firebase runs accounts, profiles, reviews and the leaderboard (only when you use them).
  • Some games load extra files from other websites (for example, Power Hungry downloads a world map).

Advertising

Visitors in some regions (like the EU and UK) are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Kids

Lots of our players are young. If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or sending a message. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent's permission. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.
