What is DORFic?

DDaylightOOrangeRRedFFuturismGGraphic

DORFic is the bright, glossy look of mid-2000s tech: white future-cities, orange swooshes, shiny glass buttons, office-tower windows and abstract tech lines. It's the orange, industrial cousin of Frutiger Aero, and it's all over old game console menus and gadgets.

This whole site is built in that style. Pick a version below, or press S anywhere to flip through them.
